# Reusable CX Knowledge Architecture — project overview

**Purpose:** Explore how important CX knowledge, rules and definitions can be governed, maintained and reused across teams, humans and AI-enabled delivery without creating competing sources of truth.

**Portfolio role:** Reusable capability exploration supporting business context and communication governance. Not a customer master, a data warehouse or a parallel knowledge authority.

## Existing work
- `00_project/`: project framing and management materials.
- `01_discover/`: discovery evidence and problem understanding.
- `02_define/`: scope and definition work.
- `03_design/`: design options and concepts.

These existing directories remain available. Their contents are not automatically approved or current; use the newest verified source and record decisions explicitly.
