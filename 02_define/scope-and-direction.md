## Candidate scope

The following are hypotheses only.

Potential reusable knowledge types may include:

- CX principles;
- customer-language rules;
- journey-status patterns;
- routing principles;
- service patterns;
- interaction rules;
- design guidance;
- implementation guidance;
- acceptance criteria.

## Out of scope

To be confirmed with the working group.

Potential exclusions include:

- raw operational data;
- customer records;
- controlled records;
- general organisational documentation;
- project-management material;
- knowledge that already has an appropriate authoritative system.

## Strategic direction

To be confirmed.

Possible directions include:

### Governed knowledge

Improve ownership, authority, lifecycle and findability.

### Reusable knowledge

Create knowledge that can be applied consistently across multiple projects and contexts.

### Strategy-to-delivery knowledge

Improve how CX intent translates into design, development and validation.

### Machine-usable knowledge

Explore whether selected knowledge can also safely support retrieval, automation, AI or software-delivery workflows.

These directions are not mutually exclusive.

## Success criteria

To be agreed with the working group.

Prefer measures that demonstrate:

- reduced duplication;
- reduced repeated interpretation;
- improved consistency;
- stronger traceability;
- improved findability;
- better implementation outcomes;
- lower recurring maintenance.

## Next decision

Select one small real-world experiment after scope and strategic direction have been sufficiently defined.
